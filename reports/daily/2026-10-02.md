### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks - Acquisition and Holding of Shares or Voting Rights) Amendment Directions, 2026

**What happened:**

RBI/2026-27/276 DOR.HOL.REC.No.236/16.13.100/2026-27 October 1, 2026 Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks - Acquisition and Holding of Shares or Voting Rights) Amendment Directions, 2026 The Reserve Bank had issued the Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Acquisition and Holding of Shares or Voting Rights) Directions, 2025 (hereinafter referred as the ‘Master Direction’), on November 28, 2025, which mandates that any person seeking to make an initial acquisition of major shareholding in a banking company shall obtain prior approval of the Reserve Bank. Further, where such person’s aggregate shareholding, after the initial acquisition of major shareholding, falls below five percent at any point of time, prior approval of the Reserve Bank shall again be required before any subsequent acquisition of major shareholding. While obtaining prior approval shall continue to be mandatory for initial acquisition of major shareholding in a banking company, based on a review, it has now been decided to grant one-time approval for subsequent acquisitions of major shareholding in the same banking company by mutual funds, insurance companies and pension funds, subject to certain requirements. Accordingly, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sections 12, 12B, and 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, the Reserve Bank being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest so to do, hereby, issues the Amendment Directions hereinafter specified.

**Why it matters:**

This matters to banks, schools, insurers, pension funds because it may change prudential, conduct, disclosure, onboarding or customer-protection duties in financial services. Teams should check scope, commencement and transition provisions against the source document. Implementation owners should map the instrument against existing policies, products and controls.

**Type:** Regulation

**Status:** Circular issued

**Effective date:** 2026-10-01

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As Applicable to Co-operative Societies) – The Industrial Co-operative Bank Ltd., Guwahati – Extension of period

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India issued Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to The Industrial Co-operative Bank Ltd., Guwahati vide Directive No.GWH.DOS.ADM.No.S103/01-10-101/2025-2026 dated July 03, 2025 for a period of six months up to close of business on January 04, 2026, which was last extended up to close of business on October 04, 2026, vide Directive No.DOR.MON./D-18/12-29-104/2026-2027 dated June 25, 2026. The Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of operation of the Directive beyond close of business on October 04, 2026. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, hereby extends the Directions for a further period of three months from the close of business on October 04, 2026 to the close of business on January 04, 2027, subject to review. The aforesaid extension and /or modification by the Reserve Bank of India should not per-se be construed to imply that the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied with the financial position of the bank. Other terms and conditions of the Directive under reference shall remain unchanged.
