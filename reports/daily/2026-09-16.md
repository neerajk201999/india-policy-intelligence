### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — Govt says there will be no charges at all for person-to-person UPI transactions, irrespective of amount transferred

**What happened:**

Government has clarified that there will be no charges at all for person-to-person (P2P) UPI transactions, irrespective of the amount transferred. Finance Ministry said that payments to merchants up to two thousand rupees, along with transactions covered under the zero Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) framework for small merchants, will also remain free. The Ministry said that consequently, approximately 96 percent of all P2M transactions will remain unaffected. It said that MDR will apply only to specified merchant transactions above two thousand rupees. The Ministry said that MDR is neither a tax nor a charge collected by the government or the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It is distributed among payment ecosystem participants, including banks and payment application providers, to support the operation and continued expansion of the UPI ecosystem. NPCI has said that a revised UPI Merchant Discount Rate framework has been introduced on select merchant transactions, effective from 15th of next month, while keeping UPI free for consumers. Akashavni correspondent reports said that a dedicated fund for promoting use of UPI by small merchants will be set up with a contribution of 5 percent of total MDR collections. The Finance Ministry said that charges will apply only to person-to-merchant (P2M) transactions exceeding two thousand rupees.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — India’s WPI based inflation rises to 9.92 % in August

**What happened:**

India’s Wholesale Price Index (WPI) based inflation rose marginally to 9.92 per cent in August, from 9.78 per cent in July, on an annual basis. According to data released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, the annual inflation last month was driven by higher prices of mineral oils, food articles, food products, basic metals, non-food articles, chemicals and chemical products. The wholesale price inflation remained above the nine percent mark for the fourth consecutive month. Among the major categories in WPI, year over year inflation in the Fuel and Power group surged to 22.9 per cent last month, from 20 per cent in July. Inflation in Manufactured Products rose slightly to almost 8.4 per cent, from 8.3 per cent. Meanwhile, inflation in Primary Articles decreased to around 7.8 per cent in August, from 8.5 per cent in July. The WPI Food Index, which comprises food articles and food products, rose to 7.05 per cent in August, from 6.65 per cent in the previous month. The Output Producer Price Index increased by 9.81 per cent year-on-year in August.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · DATA] — India’s retail inflation accelerates to 4.82 % in August

**What happened:**

India’s retail inflation accelerated to 4.82 per cent in August on an annual basis, remaining above the Reserve Bank of India’s target of four per cent for the third consecutive month this year. The August inflation reading was higher than the 4.45 per cent recorded in July as food prices remained elevated. According to data released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, inflation in rural areas increased to 5.23 per cent, while in urban regions it rose to 4.31 per cent. The food inflation rate, based on the All India Consumer Food Price Index, stood at 5.95 per cent last month, up from 5.52 per cent in the preceding month. According to the data, the increase in inflation during the last month was mainly attributed to rising prices in personal care, social protection, restaurants and accommodation services, food, beverages and intoxicants. The top items showing high inflation at the all-India combined level included Silver, Gold, Diamond and Platinum Jewellery, Ginger, Onion and Garlic. In contrast, items with lowest inflation included Tomato, Potato, Car, Lady’s finger, Parwal and Kundru.
