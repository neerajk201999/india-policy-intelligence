### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — Master Circular - Credit Facilities to Scheduled Castes (SCs) & Scheduled Tribes (STs)

**What happened:**

Circular - Credit facilities to Scheduled Castes (SCs) & Scheduled Tribes (STs) The Reserve Bank of India has, from time to time, issued a number of guidelines/instructions to banks on credit facilities to Scheduled Castes (SCs) & Scheduled Tribes (STs). The enclosed Master Circular consolidates the circulars issued by Reserve Bank on the subject till date, as listed in the Appendix . Yours faithfully, (R. Giridharan) Chief General Manager Master Circular - Credit Facilities to Scheduled Castes (SCs) & Scheduled Tribes (STs) Banks should take the measures indicated below to step up their advances to SCs/STs. Planning Process 1.1 The District Level Consultative Committees formed under the Lead Bank Scheme should continue to be the principal mechanism of co-ordination between banks and development agencies in this regard. The district credit plans formulated by the Lead Banks should clearly indicate the linkage of credit with employment and development schemes. 1.2 Banks will have to establish closer liaison with the District Industries Centres, which have been set up in different districts for promoting self-employment. 1.3 At the block level, a certain weightage is to be given to SCs/STs in the planning process. Accordingly, the credit planning should be weighted in their favour and special bankable schemes suited to them should be drawn up to ensure their participation and larger flow of credit to them for self-employment.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — India’s registered manufacturing sector output rises 7.81% in FY25

**What happened:**

India’s registered manufacturing sector recorded strong growth in financial year 2025 (FY25), with industrial output rising 7.81 per cent and total employment increasing almost 7.2 per cent over the previous year. According to the Annual Survey of Industries data released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation today, the total number of establishments in this sector increased from 2.60 lakh in FY24 to 2.67 lakh in FY25, representing a growth of 2.64 per cent. The highest number of establishments was reported in Tamil Nadu, followed by Gujarat and Maharashtra. Meanwhile, total emoluments paid to the workforce also increased by over 12 per cent in 2024-25 on an annual basis. Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, and Haryana have emerged as the top 5 states in employment, having a share of more than 56 per cent of the total manufacturing employment of the country in FY25.
