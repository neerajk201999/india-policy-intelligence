### [DIGITAL ECONOMY & AI · PROGRAMME] — Union Minister J P Nadda launches training programme for drug de-addiction professionals

**What happened:**

Union Minister of Health and Family Welfare J.P. Nadda today launched the Capacity Building Training Programme for Medical and Allied Healthcare Professionals engaged in drug de-addiction services in New Delhi. The initiative aims to strengthen the capacity of healthcare professionals in the prevention, identification, assessment, treatment and management of substance use disorders across the country. Addressing the gathering, Mr Nadda appreciated the comprehensive training programme, which includes both online and offline training for doctors, medical officers, nurses, counsellors and other healthcare professionals. He said the programme would be implemented in phases, in collaboration with regional and national institutions, before being expanded across States and Union Territories. The Union Minister reiterated the government’s commitment to achieving a Nasha Mukt Bharat by 2029, under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi. He stressed the need for a coordinated, whole-of-government approach involving various Ministries and Departments. Speaking on the occasion, Health Secretary Punya Salila Srivastava emphasised the need to protect the country’s youth from substance abuse. She said ensuring that young people remain physically healthy, mentally well and confident is essential to achieving the vision of a Viksit Bharat. According to the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, the capacity-building initiative is expected to improve access to quality, evidence-based treatment services and strengthen the national effort towards a drug-free India.

### [DEREGULATION & EASE OF DOING BUSINESS · INSTITUTIONAL] — CCPA Steps Up Action Against Misleading Walkie-Talkie Listings

**What happened:**

The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) has intensified action against misleading advertisements and non-compliant sale of walkie-talkies. The CCPA has imposed a penalty of one lakh rupees on a private company for misleading advertisements and product listings of walkie-talkies that failed to disclose essential regulatory information, including operating frequencies and applicable licensing requirements. The Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution said that the action forms part of the CCPA’s ongoing enforcement measures against misleading advertisements and unfair trade practices. It added that the CCPA had earlier taken suo motu cognisance of the large-scale listing and sale of walkie-talkies on e-commerce platforms without mandatory regulatory disclosures. During its examination, over sixteen thousand non-compliant listings were identified across platforms, and notices were issued to 13 e-commerce entities. The Ministry also informed that the Authority has so far passed final orders against 12 entities, imposing cumulative penalties of 58 lakh rupees for misleading advertisements and unfair trade practices relating to walkie-talkies.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — Country’s FOREX reserves decline by $5bn to $721bn

**What happened:**

The country’s foreign exchange reserves declined by five billion US dollars to 721 billion US dollars during the week ended on 16th October. According to the Reserve Bank of India’s data, Foreign currency assets, the largest component of the reserves, dropped by 5.7 billion US dollars to 591.8 US dollars during the week. In contrast, Gold reserves rose by 706 million US dollars to 105.8 billion dollars. Special Drawing Rights grew by 30 million US dollars to 18.5 billion US dollars. The country’s reserve position with the International Monetary Fund rose 11 million to 4.79 billion dollars.
