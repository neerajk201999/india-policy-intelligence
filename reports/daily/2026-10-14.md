### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · CONSULTATION] — Government drafts National Steel Policy-Vision 2047 to transform steel sector

**What happened:**

The Government has the draft National Steel Policy-Vision 2047, which provides a long-term policy framework to build a globally competitive, technologically advanced, environmentally sustainable and resource-efficient steel sector. The Policy aims to meet the country’s growing steel requirements while strengthening India’s position in the global steel industry. The policy projects the country’s per capita steel consumption rising to 302 kilograms from the current 116 kilograms, while India’s crude steel production has already increased from 89 million tonnes in 2014-15 to 170 million tonnes in 2025-26. The Policy also projects an increase in the country’s steel exports to 45 MT by 2047. Further, it says that India aims for a near threefold expansion in crude steel-making capacity to 604 million tonnes by 2047 and to raise finished steel demand to 505 million tonnes. The draft Policy focuses on promoting greater use of steel in infrastructure, housing, transportation, renewable energy, and emerging manufacturing sectors. It also places emphasis on cleaner steel production, seeking to lower average emissions intensity from 2.54 tonnes of carbon dioxide per tonne of crude steel to 1.54 tonnes by 2047 through measures including greater energy efficiency, renewable electricity, increased scrap use and hydrogen-based steel-making.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · DATA] — IMF raises India’s GDP growth forecast to 7% for 2026-27

**What happened:**

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has raised India’s GDP growth forecast to 7 per cent for 2026-27, up from 6.4 per cent in July. In its flagship World Economic Outlook report, the IMF said the revision in the estimate comes on the back of stronger-than-expected momentum, supported by resilient domestic demand and robust services activity. It said India stands out with upward revisions, reflecting sustained momentum and growing evidence of stronger growth potential supported by favourable demographic dynamics, an expanding domestic market, and continued structural reforms. The revision came after the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) latest monetary policy meeting, which raised its GDP growth estimate for the current financial year to 7.1 per cent. The IMF has also lifted its FY28 growth projection for India by 0.3 percentage points to 7%, pointing to the durability of the country’s expansion.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — RBI Tightens Rupee Derivatives Rules, Cuts Dollar Transaction Limit to $5 Million

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has introduced fresh restrictions on rupee derivatives to curb currency speculation and maintain an orderly foreign exchange market. The central bank has reduced the threshold for foreign exchange derivative transactions without underlying exposure from 100 million to 5 million dollars across authorised dealers and recognised stock exchanges. Under the new directions, authorised dealers will not be allowed to rebook rupee-involving foreign exchange derivative contracts cancelled after the issuance of the directions. However, rollover of contracts upon maturity will continue to be permitted. The RBI has also directed users hedging contracted exposures to confirm that the same underlying exposure has not been hedged with another authorised dealer. Additionally, the RBI has introduced a Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR), requiring authorised dealers to maintain a cash reserve equivalent to 20 per cent of the rupee value of qualifying derivative contracts exceeding $2 million that hedge current-account exposures involving the purchase of foreign currency against the rupee. The reserve must be maintained daily until the contract is terminated. The central bank has warned that attempts to circumvent the requirements through multiple transactions will be treated as violations. Authorised dealers must also report FERR details daily through the Centralised Information Management System.
