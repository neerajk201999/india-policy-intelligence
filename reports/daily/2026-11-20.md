### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — NHAI clarifies current SMS notifications regarding vehicle insurance renewal are purely advisory in nature

**What happened:**

National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) has clarified that SMS notifications currently being received by select National Highway users regarding vehicle insurance renewal are part of an ongoing testing process for a proposed public awareness initiative. NHAI emphasizes that the initiative is purely informational and advisory in nature. The authority said that there is no provision for issuing challans or imposing any penalties at toll plazas in connection with these alerts. It added that the notifications are intended simply to alert vehicle owners about the upcoming expiry dates of their motor insurance policies, encouraging timely renewal to ensure continuous coverage.

### [COMPETITION · INSTITUTIONAL] — India must emerge as global manufacturing and export hub for oil and gas equipment, says Hardeep Puri

**What happened:**

Petroleum and Natural Gas Minister Hardeep Singh Puri has called for greater indigenisation and self-reliance in the country’s oil and gas equipment sector to strengthen domestic manufacturing and boost exports. During an industry interaction with members of the Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FICCI) in New Delhi yesterday, the Minister stressed the need to develop and manufacture advanced technologies and equipment within the country. He emphasised that India must move beyond import substitution towards developing world-class indigenous technologies, strengthening domestic supply chains and emerging as a global manufacturing and export hub for oil and gas equipment. He said country’s expanding energy infrastructure offers significant opportunities for domestic manufacturers. The Minister said that initiatives such as GOBARdhan, and the Samudra Manthan Mission are expected to open up new opportunities in Compressed Bio-Gas, offshore exploration, specialised equipment and advanced engineering. Mr Puri called upon Oil and Gas Public Sector Undertakings to partner with Indian manufacturers, promote technology development, facilitate field trials and provide greater opportunities to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — India’s electronics exports grow 44% to $32 bn during first half of this fiscal

**What happened:**

India’s electronic goods exports registered strong growth of 44 per cent during April-September this fiscal year, increasing from over 22 billion dollars in the corresponding period of 2025-26 to around 32 billion dollars. While smartphones continued to account for the largest share of the electronics export basket, non-smartphone electronics recorded significantly faster growth, reflecting the broadening and diversification of India’s electronics exports across components, printed circuit boards, communication equipment, memory products and storage devices. Exports of smartphones increased by over 35 per cent, from 13 billion dollars in April-September last year to over 18 billion dollars during April-September this year. Smartphones continued to be the largest component of India’s electronic goods exports. However, their share in total electronic goods exports moderated from 60.3 per cent to 56.8 per cent, amid faster growth in other electronics segments. Non-smartphone electronics exports recorded a growth of over 56 per cent, increasing from around nine billion dollars to over 13 billion dollars during the period. The share of non-smartphone electronics in total electronic goods exports increased from 39.7 per cent to 43.2 per cent, indicating a broadening of India’s electronics export basket. Exports of parts and accessories comprising mounted printed circuit boards increased from over 63 million dollars to over two billion dollars registering growth of 3219 per cent.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Nagar Sahakari Bank Ltd., Etawah – Extension of Period

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India issued Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to Nagar Sahakari Bank Ltd., Etawah, vide Directive No. LKO.DOS.SED.No.S29/12-03-000/2026-2027 dated May 15, 2026, for a period of six months up to close of business on November 18, 2026. The Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of operation of the Directive beyond close of business on November 18, 2026. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India, in the exercise of the powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, hereby extends the Directive for a further period of three months from close of business on November 18, 2026, to close of business on February 18, 2027, subject to review. The aforesaid extension and/or modification by the Reserve Bank of India should not per se be construed to imply that the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.
