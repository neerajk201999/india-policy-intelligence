### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · CONSULTATION] — FM Sitharaman hold final Pre-Budget consultation meeting with youth

**What happened:**

Union Minister of Finance Nirmala Sitharaman has concluded the extensive pre-budget consultation meetings for the preparation of the Union Budget 2027-28. Ms Sitharaman today chaired the 13th and final Pre-Budget Consultation with youth for the upcoming Union Budget 2027-28, in New Delhi. The first consultation meeting was held on 16th of this month. Ministry of Finance has said that more than 200 stakeholders representing 13 sectors and stakeholder groups participated in the consultations. These include industry associations, domain experts, youth representatives and practitioners from Agriculture and Rural Development; Manufacturing, MSMEs and Startups; Capital Markets and Banking, Financial Services and Insurance, Information Technology, Infrastructure, Energy and Sustainability and Employment and Labour.The consultation meeting with youth representatives was a novel initiative this year. The Ministry said that the the consultations generated a wide range of forward-looking recommendations aimed at strengthening India’s human capital, innovation ecosystem, manufacturing competitiveness, agricultural productivity, housing security, and educational outcomes. Stakeholders emphasized the need for reforms that can further accelerate economic growth while ensuring broad-based and inclusive development.Collectively, the recommendations focused on promoting innovation-led growth, expanding employment opportunities, strengthening social and economic infrastructure, and building a more resilient and future-ready economy. The pre-budget consultations form an important part of the Government’s participatory and consultative approach to budget-making, enabling diverse stakeholders to contribute to shaping India’s development priorities and growth agenda.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — 47th Half-Yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves: April – September 2026

**What happened:**

It may be recalled that in February 2004 , the Reserve Bank initiated the practice of compiling half-yearly reports on the management of foreign exchange reserves and making them public to promote greater transparency and enhance disclosure in the management of the country’s foreign exchange reserves. In this connection, Reserve Bank of India has today released the 47th Half-Yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves with reference to end-September 2026. During April-September 2026, India’s foreign exchange reserves increased from US$ 691.11 billion to US$ 737.78 billion.
