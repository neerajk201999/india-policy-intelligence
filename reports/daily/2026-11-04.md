### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · DATA] — RBI launches the November 2026 round of the Inflation Expectations Survey of Households

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India has been regularly conducting Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH). The November 2026 round of the survey is being launched. The survey aims at capturing subjective assessments on price movements and inflation, based on their individual consumption baskets, across 19 cities, viz., Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Chandigarh, Chennai, Delhi, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Jammu, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mumbai, Nagpur, Patna, Raipur, Ranchi and Thiruvananthapuram. The survey seeks qualitative responses from households on price changes (general prices as well as prices of specific product groups) in the three months ahead as well as in the one year ahead period and quantitative responses on current, three months ahead and one year ahead inflation rates. The results of this survey provide useful inputs for monetary policy. The agency, M/s Hansa Research Group Pvt. ### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · DATA] — RBI simplifies forex reporting rules for small-value service exports and imports

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has simplified foreign exchange reporting rules for service exporters and importers, easing compliance requirements for individuals and small-value transactions. Under the revised regulations, resident individuals exporting services will no longer need to submit a separate Export Declaration Form. Providing payment details to their authorised dealer bank will be sufficient. The same facility will apply to other exporters for inward remittances of up to ten lakh rupees. For imports of services, banks will be required to report transactions in the Import Data Processing and Monitoring System only when the importer is not a resident individual and the invoice or contract value exceeds ten lakh rupees. The amendments, notified on 2nd November, came into effect from 1st October 2026.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · INSTITUTIONAL] — NITI Aayog launches India Science and Technology Scoreboard to assess performance of States and UTs

**What happened:**

NITI Aayog today launched the India Science and Technology (S&T) Scoreboard to assess the scientific and technological performance of States and Union Territories across the country in New Delhi today. The scoreboard was launched by NITI Aayog Vice Chairman Ashok Kumar Lahiri. The platform aims to provide a comprehensive picture of India’s science and technology ecosystem through key indicators, including research and development expenditure, scientific publications, citations, patent applications and technological innovation. The platform covers 36 States and Union Territories and provides data and insights to support evidence-based policymaking. The initiative is expected to promote collaboration between state universities and centrally funded research institutions, encourage healthy competition and strengthen India’s research and innovation ecosystem in line with the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047. Speaking at the event, Ashok Kumar Lahiri said that the scoreboard is not merely a ranking exercise but a platform to help states understand their strengths and gaps and align their developmental priorities with science, technology and innovation. He also emphasised the importance of translational research, stating that fundamental research must translate into innovation and reach the factory floor. He called upon states to take ownership of scientific and technological development and strengthen their innovation ecosystems.
