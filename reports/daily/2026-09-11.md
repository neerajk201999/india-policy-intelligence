### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — RBI imposes monetary penalty on Jilla Sahakari Bank Ltd., Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has, by an order dated September 09, 2026, imposed a monetary penalty of ₹1.15 lakh (Rupees One Lakh and Fifteen Thousand only) on Jilla Sahakari Bank Ltd., Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh (the bank) for non-compliance with certain directions issued by RBI on ‘Membership of Credit Information Companies (CICs) by Co-operative Banks’ and ‘Know Your Customer (KYC)’. This penalty has been imposed in exercise of powers conferred on RBI under the provisions of Section 25(1)(iii) read with Section 23(4) of Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005 and under Section 47A(1)(c) read with Sections 46(4)(i) and 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The statutory inspection of the bank was conducted by National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) with reference to its financial position as on March 31, 2025. Based on supervisory findings of non-compliance with RBI directions and related correspondence in that regard, a notice was issued to the bank advising it to show cause as to why penalty should not be imposed on it for its failure to comply with the said directions. After considering the bank’s reply to the notice, additional submissions made by it and oral submissions made during the personal hearing, RBI found, inter alia, that the following charges against the bank were sustained, warranting imposition of monetary penalty: The bank had: failed to report credit information of its borrowers to all Credit Information Companies; and failed to upload the KYC records of customers onto Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR).

### [DEREGULATION & EASE OF DOING BUSINESS · REGULATION] — Centre amends e-commerce rules to protect consumers and enhance transparency

**What happened:**

The government has amended the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, to tighten norms on price transparency, sponsored listings, search result manipulation and dark patterns. Under the amended rules, e-commerce entities will have to disclose the reduced price and prior price whenever a price cut is announced. The prior price has been defined as the lowest price at which the product was offered in the 30 days before the discount was announced. The rules bar e-commerce entities from manipulating search results that mislead users or affect the relevance of results to a search query. The amended rules will establish a more transparent, accountable and consumer-centric e-commerce ecosystem. The amended rules will come into force from 1st January next year. The Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution said that the amended rules will strengthen the ease of doing business by curtailing safety issues around digital marketplace. It also highlighted that every e-commerce entity will be required to become a partner in the convergence process of the National Consumer Helpline providing an inclusive grievance redressal mechanism. Under the new rules, every e-commerce entity shall provide the complainant with a copy of the complaint as recorded by its grievance officer. E-commerce entities will not manipulate search results in a manner that misleads users or adversely affects the relevance of results to the user’s search query.

### [DIGITAL ECONOMY & AI · INSTITUTIONAL] — Delhi Govt approves introduction of ‘Grassroot Training Support Assistance’ under Mukhyamantri Khel Protsahan Yojana

**What happened:**

The Delhi Government has approved the introduction of a ‘Grassroot Training Support Assistance’ component under the Mukhyamantri Khel Protsahan Yojana. Under the new initiative, eligible athletes training regularly at Delhi government sports centres and stadiums will receive financial assistance of two thousand rupees per month. The scheme will cover athletes in the Under-14, Under-17, Under-19 and Under-21 categories. The assistance will help athletes meet expenses related to nutrition, local travel, basic sports equipment and medical needs. Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta said that the assistance will help young athletes continue their training without interruption and meet expenses related to nutrition, local travel, basic sporting equipment and medical needs. Delhi’s Sports Minister Ashish Sood said the initiative aims to ensure that financial difficulties do not force talented young athletes to give up their sporting ambitions.
