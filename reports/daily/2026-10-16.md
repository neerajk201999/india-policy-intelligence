### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — Wholesale Price Index inflation rises to 11.39% in September this year

**What happened:**

India’s Wholesale Price Index (WPI) based inflation rose sharply to 11.39% in September, from 9.92% in August, on an annual basis. According to the provisional data released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, the annual inflation last month was driven by higher prices of mineral oils, food articles, crude petroleum and natural gas, coal and lignite, basic metals, chemicals and chemical products, textiles, rubber and plastic products and electrical equipment. Among the major categories in WPI, year-on-year inflation in the Fuel and Power group surged to 25.85% in September, from 22.93% in August. Inflation in Manufactured Products rose to 9.76%, from 8.37%, and inflation in Primary Articles edged up to 8.67%, from 7.76%, during the same period. The WPI Food Index, which comprises food articles and food products, rose to 8.66% in September, from 7.05% in August. The Output Producer Price Index increased by 11.38% year-on-year in September. According to the data, cumulative WPI inflation during the first half of this financial year stood at 9.85%, compared with a contraction of 0.2% during the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · DATA] — IMF raises India’s GDP growth forecast to 7% for 2026-27

**What happened:**

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has raised India’s GDP growth forecast to 7 per cent for 2026-27, up from 6.4 per cent in July. In its flagship World Economic Outlook report, the IMF said the revision in the estimate comes on the back of stronger-than-expected momentum, supported by resilient domestic demand and robust services activity. The revision came after the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) latest monetary policy meeting, which raised its GDP growth estimate for the current financial year to 7.1 per cent.
