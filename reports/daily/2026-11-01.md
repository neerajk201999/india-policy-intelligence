### [DIGITAL ECONOMY & AI · INSTITUTIONAL] — PM Modi approves northern bullet train corridor linking Delhi to Guwahati via Siliguri

**What happened:**

PM addresses at the release of instalment under the Annapurna Yojana in West Bengal via video conferencing on September 17, 2026. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has approved the Northern Bullet Train Corridor, which will connect Delhi to Guwahati via Siliguri. This was disclosed by Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw in Varanasi yesterday. The Minister was in the city to flag off the Varanasi-New Delhi Vande Bharat Sleeper Express. Mr Vaishnaw said the Northern Bullet Train Corridor aims to revolutionise connectivity across northern and eastern India, cutting down the current 20-hour journey between Delhi and Siliguri to just six hours. The high-speed rail corridor will cover approximately 1,500 km and will pass through key cities across Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and West Bengal before extending into Assam. The key stations include Delhi, Agra, Kanpur, Lucknow, Ayodhya, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Patna, Siliguri, and Guwahati. Mr Vaishnaw said that ticket fares will be highly economical and significantly lower than airfares. He said the high-speed rail network is designed to boost small-scale industries, directly benefiting middle-income households, youth, and local artisans.

### [DEREGULATION & EASE OF DOING BUSINESS · INSTITUTIONAL] — US approves $2.1 billion Patriot missile deal for Ukraine; Zelenskyy welcomes approval

**What happened:**

The United States has approved a potential sale of 500 Patriot PAC-3 Missile Segment Enhancement (MSE) interceptors to Ukraine at an estimated cost of 2.1 billion US dollars. The acquisition will be funded entirely by European partners and Canada, with no financial contribution from the United States. The US State Department formally announced the approval yesterday, stating that the proposed sale aims to strengthen Ukraine’s air defence capabilities and protect it against current and emerging missile threats. Canada, Germany and Norway are expected to contribute towards the purchase through the US JUMPSTART programme, along with financial support from a European Union credit facility. US defence company Lockheed Martin will serve as the primary contractor for the proposed deal. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has welcomed the US State Department’s approval of the procurement of Patriot missile interceptors to counter incoming Russian ballistic missile attacks. In a social media post, Mr Zelenskyy described the approval as a “strong decision”. He said the 500 PAC-3 interceptors would significantly strengthen Ukraine’s air defence capabilities and could provide an effective defence package during the winter months. However, he urged Washington to expedite their delivery in view of Ukraine’s immediate security needs during the winter season.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — Forex reserves decline over $1.2bn last week

**What happened:**

The country’s foreign exchange reserves declined over 1.2 billion US dollars to 719.7 billion US dollars during the week ended on 23rd of this month. According to the Reserve Bank of India’s data, Gold reserves dropped by 2.6 billion US dollars to 103 billion US dollars. Country’s reserve position with the International Monetary Fund fell four million to 4.78 billion US dollars. Special Drawing Rights grew by 27 million US dollars to 18.6 billion US dollars.
