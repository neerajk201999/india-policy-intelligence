### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · CONSULTATION] — RBI Issues Amendment Directions on ‘Suspected Money Mule Accounts to Prevent Cyber-enabled Financial Frauds’

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank had issued the draft Reserve Bank of India (Know Your Customer) Amendment Directions, 2026 on September 11, 2026, for seeking feedback from stakeholders. The draft Amendment Directions proposed a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) laying down a uniform, time-bound procedure for banks to suo moto place temporary debit holds on amounts or accounts suspected to be linked to money mule activity and cyber-enabled financial frauds, and to remove such holds in coordination with Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs), while protecting genuine customers from inconvenience. Feedback received on the above draft Directions has been examined and the consequent modifications have been suitably incorporated in the final Amendment Directions. A statement on the feedback received is provided in the Annex . Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today issued the following Amendment Directions, which shall come into effect from July 1, 2027, or from such earlier date as a bank may decide to implement the SOP. ### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — India’s forex reserves decline by 4.4 billion dollars to 700.9 billion dollars

**What happened:**

Country’s foreign exchange reserves declined by 4.4 billion US dollars to 700.9 billion US dollars during the week ended on 20th of this month. According to the Reserve Bank of India’s data, Gold reserves dropped over 4 billion US dollars to 94.5 billion US dollars during the week. Foreign currency assets, the largest component of the reserves, slipped by 266 million US dollars to 583.2 billion US dollars. Special Drawing Rights fell by 67 million US dollars to 18.45 billion US dollars. Country’s reserve position with the International Monetary Fund lost 15 million US dollars to 4.74 billion US dollars.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · CONSULTATION] — FM Sitharaman hold final Pre-Budget consultation meeting with youth

**What happened:**

Union Minister of Finance Nirmala Sitharaman has concluded the extensive pre-budget consultation meetings for the preparation of the Union Budget 2027-28. Ms Sitharaman today chaired the 13th and final Pre-Budget Consultation with youth for the upcoming Union Budget 2027-28, in New Delhi. The first consultation meeting was held on 16th of this month. Ministry of Finance has said that more than 200 stakeholders representing 13 sectors and stakeholder groups participated in the consultations. These include industry associations, domain experts, youth representatives and practitioners from Agriculture and Rural Development; Manufacturing, MSMEs and Startups; Capital Markets and Banking, Financial Services and Insurance, Information Technology, Infrastructure, Energy and Sustainability and Employment and Labour.The consultation meeting with youth representatives was a novel initiative this year. The Ministry said that the the consultations generated a wide range of forward-looking recommendations aimed at strengthening India’s human capital, innovation ecosystem, manufacturing competitiveness, agricultural productivity, housing security, and educational outcomes. Stakeholders emphasized the need for reforms that can further accelerate economic growth while ensuring broad-based and inclusive development.Collectively, the recommendations focused on promoting innovation-led growth, expanding employment opportunities, strengthening social and economic infrastructure, and building a more resilient and future-ready economy. The pre-budget consultations form an important part of the Government’s participatory and consultative approach to budget-making, enabling diverse stakeholders to contribute to shaping India’s development priorities and growth agenda.
