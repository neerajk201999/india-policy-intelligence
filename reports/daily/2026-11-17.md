### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — Oil, metals & chemical products key drivers of wholesale price inflation during September

**What happened:**

India’s Wholesale Price Index (WPI) based inflation rose to 12.29 per cent in October on an annual basis, from 11.39 per cent in September. According to the provisional data released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, crude petroleum and natural gas, mineral oils, basic metals, chemicals and chemical products were among the key drivers of wholesale price inflation during the month. Among the major categories in WPI, year-on-year inflation in the Fuel and Power surged sharply to 32.84 per cent last month. Inflation in Primary Articles rose to 9.24 per cent, from 8.67 per cent in September, while inflation in Manufactured Products eased marginally to 9.54 per cent during the same period. However, the WPI Food Index, which comprises food articles and food products, dipped marginally to 8.58 per cent last month, from 8.66 per cent September. Meanwhile, the Output Producer Price Index grew by 12.06 per cent year-on-year in October. According to the data, cumulative WPI inflation for April to October period stood at 10.14 per cent, compared with a contraction of 0.3 per cent during the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — Niveshak Shivir helps investors and shareholders resolve issues

**What happened:**

The Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA), Securities and Exchange Board of India and Market Infrastructure Institutions will orgaise a day-long Niveshak Shivir in New Delhi tomorrow. Ministry of Corporate Affairs said that the initiative is designed to help investors and shareholders resolve issues related to unclaimed dividends, shares transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund and other claimant-related grievances. Through the Shivir, IEPFA aims to facilitate the recovery of unclaimed dividends and shares held for six to seven years with companies, on-the-spot KYC updation and nomination-related services. It will also provide immediate assistance and resolution of pending IEPFA claim-related issues. The Niveshak Shivir model promotes direct, intermediary-free engagement between investors, companies, and Registrars and Transfer Agents. The Niveshak Shivir forms part of IEPFA’s nationwide investor outreach and awareness programme, aimed at strengthening financial literacy, enhancing transparency, and simplifying the process of reclaiming unclaimed investments.
