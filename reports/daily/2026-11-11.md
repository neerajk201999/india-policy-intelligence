### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — Auction of Government of India Dated Security

**What happened:**

Government of India (GoI) has announced the sale (re-issue) of one dated security for a notified amount of ₹34,000 crore as per the following details: Sr No Security Date of Repayment Notified Amount (₹ Crore) GoI specific Notification Auction Date Settlement Date 1 6.94% GS 2036 May 11, 2036 34,000 F.No.4(1)-B(W&M)/2026 dated November 09, 2026 November 13, 2026 (Friday) November 16, 2026 (Monday) Total 34,000 2. GoI will have the option to retain additional subscription up to ₹2,000 crore against the security mentioned above. The security will be sold through Reserve Bank of India Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai – 400001. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions spelt out in the ‘Specific Notification’ mentioned above and the General Notification F.No.4(2)–B(W&M)/2018, dated March 26, 2025 . The auction will be conducted using multiple price method . Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber system) on November 13, 2026 (Friday) . The non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10:30 a.m. and the competitive bids should be submitted between 10:30 a.m. The result will be announced on the same day and payment by successful bidders will have to be made on November 16, 2026 (Monday) .

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — Nvidia launches probe into reports of foreign intelligence effort targeting CEO

**What happened:**

American multinational technology company, Nvidia is opening an investigation into reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. The company’s board of directors made the announcement after Huang acknowledged to the board that he had been intimate with a business executive Alice Huanga, a Chinese citizen and chip buyer, whom US intelligence determined was gathering information for Beijing. Huang apologised in a message to the company, saying he was unfaithful to his wife and his actions hurt her and their children for which he is deeply sorry. Nvidia’s board said in a statement it has no reason to believe that either national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, but that its inquiry would examine all available information. Huang fully supports the review and has committed to cooperating with it, the board said. Last month, US intelligence agencies had discovered that Huang had developed a close relationship with Alice Huang and determined that she was feeding information directly to Beijing.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Sri Guru Raghavendra Sahakara Bank Niyamitha, Bengaluru - Extension of Period

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India issued Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to Sri Guru Raghavendra Sahakara Bank Niyamitha, Bengaluru vide Directive No. DoS.CO.UCB.BSD-III.D-2/12.23.283/2019-20 dated January 02, 2020, for a period of six months up to close of business on July 10, 2020. The Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of operation of the Directive beyond close of business on November 10, 2026. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India, in the exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, hereby extends the Directive for a further period of six months from close of business on November 10, 2026 to close of business on May 10, 2027, subject to review. The aforesaid extension and /or modification by the Reserve Bank of India should not per-se be construed to imply that the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.
