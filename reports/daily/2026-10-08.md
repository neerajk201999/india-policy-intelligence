### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · CONSULTATION] — RBI issues Directions on 'Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) Framework’

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India had issued draft Directions on the ‘ Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) Framework’ on August 7, 2026 , inviting feedback from stakeholders until August 28, 2026. The draft Directions proposed modifications to the instructions on capital charge for CVA risk for commercial banks. Key revisions included: (i) allowing eligible banks to choose a simpler approach; (ii) increasing the sensitivity of supervisory risk weights for counterparties by sector and credit quality; (iii) clarifying the eligibility and recognition of CVA hedges; and (iv) separate systematic and idiosyncratic CVA risk components in the full Basic Approach (BA-CVA) calculation, addressing imperfect alignment of indirect CVA hedges. Feedback received on the draft instructions has been examined and consequent modifications have been suitably incorporated in the final Directions. Statement on the feedback received on the draft instructions is provided in the Annex . Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today released the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Credit Valuation Adjustment Framework) Directions, 2026 . These instructions will come into effect from April 1, 2027. (Brij Raj) Chief General Manager Press Release: 2026-2027/1271

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · CONSULTATION] — RBI issues Amendment Directions on 'Standardised Approach for Counterparty Credit Risk (SA-CCR)’

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India had issued draft Amendment Directions on the ‘ Standardised Approach for Counterparty Credit Risk (SA-CCR)’ on June 10, 2026 , inviting feedback from stakeholders until July 1, 2026. The draft Amendment Directions proposed modifications to the instructions on capital charge for counterparty credit risk as provided in Master Direction on Forthcoming Instructions for commercial banks. Key revisions included: (i) clarifying the scope of CCR for both banking and trading book exposures, (ii) addressing treatment of multiple margin agreements and multiple netting sets in light of recent legal / regulatory developments, (iii) providing guidance for transactions where a bank acts as a clearing member of SEBI-recognised stock exchanges in the equity derivatives and commodity derivatives segments, (iv) specifying the treatment for deferment of option premium, (v) offering guidance on computing effective notional for options, and (vi) including disclosure templates for SA-CCR. Feedback received on the draft instructions has been examined and consequent modifications have been suitably incorporated in the final Amendment Directions. Statement on the feedback received on the draft instructions is provided in the Annex . ### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — 25-basis-point repo rate hike unlikely to impact economic expansion, says RBI Governor

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) today said the recent 25-basis-point repo rate hike could moderate bank credit growth, which is currently above 18 per cent, but is unlikely to have a significant impact on overall economic expansion. RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra said credit growth is expected to remain strong and continue supporting economic activity, adding that minor fluctuations in its pace would not materially affect the economy. The RBI has shifted its policy stance to calibrated tightening and indicated that rate cuts are unlikely in the near term. Malhotra said monetary policy decisions would primarily be guided by domestic growth and inflation conditions, while global developments would also be taken into account. The Governor said the renewed escalation of the West Asia conflict, volatile crude oil prices, rising bond yields in advanced economies and an appreciating US dollar have weakened global economic sentiment and increased financial market volatility. He said global growth is expected to slow in 2026 despite remaining resilient, while higher energy and food prices are likely to push up inflation and prompt further monetary policy tightening by major central banks.
