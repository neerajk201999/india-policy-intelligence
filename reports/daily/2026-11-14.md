### [EDUCATION · DATA] — India records all-time high exports of nearly 90 billion dollars in October: Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal

**What happened:**

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said, India recorded an all-time high in exports, reaching nearly 90 billion dollars in October this year. Addressing the inaugural ceremony of the India International Trade Fair (IITF) in New Delhi yesterday, Mr Goyal said the export figure of October comprised around 50 billion dollars in merchandise exports and 40 billion dollars in services exports. He further informed that India’s cumulative exports during the first seven months of the current financial year, up to October, has reached 570 billion dollars. Expressing confidence in the country’s export performance, the Minister said India is well on course to achieve its one-trillion-dollar export target this year. Mr Goyal also expressed confidence that India’s international trade will register growth of 15 to 16 per cent during the current year, despite ongoing conflicts and global uncertainties. He said the country’s performance reflects the resilience of its economy, the confidence of its exporters and India’s ability to overcome global challenges with determination and self-respect. Highlighting the strength of India’s domestic economy, Mr Goyal referred to the strong business activity witnessed during the recent festive season, particularly around Diwali. He said markets across the country recorded robust consumer demand and increased business activity.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — Reserve Bank of India imposes monetary penalty on One Mobikwik Systems Limited

**What happened:**

Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has, by an order dated November 06, 2026, imposed a monetary penalty of ₹9.00 lakh (Rupees Nine Lakh only) on One Mobikwik Systems Limited (the company) for non-compliance with certain provisions of the directions issued by RBI on ‘Prepaid Payment lnstruments (PPIs)’ and ‘Fraud reporting’. This penalty has been imposed in exercise of powers conferred on RBI under the provisions of section 30(1) read with section 26(6) of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007. The statutory inspection of the company was conducted by RBI with reference to its operations for the period from June 2025 to March 2026. Based on supervisory findings of non-compliance with RBI directions and related correspondence in that regard, a notice was issued to the company advising it to show cause as to why penalty should not be imposed on it for its failure to comply with the said RBI directions. After considering the company’s reply to the notice, additional submissions made by it and oral submissions made during the personal hearing, RBI found that the following charges against the company were sustained, warranting imposition of monetary penalty: the company had allowed fund transfer by certain PPI holders to non-pre-registered beneficiaries beyond the prescribed limit.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — India’s merchandise exports rise to record high of $49.47bn in Oct despite global uncertainty

**What happened:**

I ndia’s merchandise exports rose by 45 per cent year-on-year to a record high of 49.47 billion US dollars in October this year despite the uncertainties in global markets. According to the data released by the Commerce and Industry Ministry, India’s total exports, including merchandise and services, surged by nearly 30 per cent year-on-year to reach 89.99 billion US dollars, while overall imports rose nearly 16.6 per cent to over 110 billion US dollars last month. Driven by a record merchandise export, the overall trade deficit narrowed to 20.09 billion US dollars. The Ministry noted that the major drivers of merchandise export growth in October included Petroleum Products, Electronic Goods, Gems and Jewellery, Organic and Inorganic Chemicals, and Engineering Goods. Meanwhile, India’s total exports, comprising merchandise and services, are estimated at over 568 billion dollars during April to October of the current financial year. This represents a growth of more than 16 per cent compared to the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — India’s forex reserves increase by $2.49bn to reach $719.54bn last week

**What happened:**

India’s forex reserves have recorded an increase of 2.49 billion to reach 719.547 billion dollars during the week ended 6th November 2026. According to an official press release issued by the Reserve Bank, the foreign currency assets, a major component of the reserves, spiked by 232 million dollars to 591.350 billion dollars. In the previous reporting week, the reserves had dropped by 2.691 billion dollars to 717.056 billion dollars. As per the statement, gold reserves jumped by 2.205 billion dollars to 104.796 billion dollars. The Special Drawing Rights – SDRs also went up by 52 million dollars to 18.610 billion dollars and India’s reserve position with the IMF also recorded a rise of 1 million dollars to 4.791 billion dollars at the end of the reporting week.
