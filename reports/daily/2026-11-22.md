### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — Core industries register growth of 10.9% in Oct due to strong performance of coal, iron sectors

**What happened:**

The combined Index of Core Industries (ICI) registered growth of 10.9 per cent in October on an annual basis driven by strong performance in iron ore, coal, electricity and cement sectors. This is higher than the upwardly revised growth rate of 8.2 per cent recorded in September. According to the provisional estimates released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry today, six out of nine core sectors recorded positive growth last month. Among these, iron ore production recorded the highest growth at 26.4 per cent. Coal production accelerated by 23.1 per cent, electricity generation increased by 21.6 per cent, cement production gained 15.4 per cent, steel production output grew by 7.3 per cent, and refinery products expanded by 3.4 per cent on an annual basis. However, natural gas production fell by 7.6 per cent, crude oil production dipped by 4.1 per cent, and fertilizer production declined by 12.2 per cent in October on an annual basis.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — RBI imposes monetary penalty on Fusion Finance Limited

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has, by an order dated November 17, 2026, imposed a monetary penalty of ₹7.50 lakh (Rupees Seven Lakh Fifty Thousand only) on Fusion Finance Limited (the company) for non-compliance with certain provisions of directions issued by RBI on ‘Know Your Customer (KYC)’ and ‘Internal Ombudsman’. This penalty has been imposed in exercise of powers conferred on RBI under section 58G(1)(b) read with section 58B(5)(aa) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. The statutory inspection of the company was conducted by RBI with reference to its financial position as on March 31, 2026. Based on the supervisory findings of non-compliance with RBI directions and related correspondence in that regard, a notice was issued to the company advising it to show cause as to why penalty should not be imposed on it for failure to comply with the RBI directions. After considering the company's reply to the notice, additional submissions made by it and oral submissions made during the personal hearing, RBI found that the following charges against the company were sustained, warranting imposition of monetary penalty: The company failed to obtain PAN or equivalent e-document thereof or Form 60 from customers, while establishing an account-based relationship with those customers; and, The company failed to escalate the partly or wholly rejected complaints to the Internal Ombudsman for a final decision within the prescribed time.

### [DEREGULATION & EASE OF DOING BUSINESS · REGULATION] — Reserve Bank issues Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026

**What happened:**

The Foreign Exchange Management (Export of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2015 were superseded by the Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026, which were issued on January 13, 2026 , and became effective from October 01, 2026. The new regulations are principle based; intended to promote ease of doing business, especially for small exporters and importers; and empower Authorised Dealers to provide quicker and more efficient service to their customers. Based on subsequent feedback received from stakeholders, the Reserve Bank has issued Amendment Regulations to Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 that were notified on November 02, 2026 , and came into effect, retrospectively, from October 01, 2026 . In this regard, to facilitate better understanding of the new trade regulations by the exporters/importers and other stakeholders, the Reserve Bank has placed a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on the Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 on its website.
