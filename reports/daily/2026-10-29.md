### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Sonpeth Nagari Sahakari Bank Maryadit, Sonpeth, Dist. Parbhani – Extension of Period

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India issued Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to Sonpeth Nagari Sahakari Bank Maryadit, Sonpeth, Dist. Parbhani, vide Directive No. NGP.DOS.SSM 3.No.S403/15-04-395/2025-2026 dated July 29, 2025, for a period of six months up to the close of business on January 30, 2026 which was last extended up to the close of business on October 30, 2026 vide Directive No. DOR.MON/D-28/12.22.718/2026-27 dated July 22, 2026. The Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of operation of the Directive beyond close of business on October 30, 2026. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India, in the exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, hereby extends the Directive for a further period of three months from close of business on October 30, 2026, to close of business on January 30, 2027, subject to review. The aforesaid extension and /or modification by the Reserve Bank of India should not per se be construed to imply that the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.

### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · CONSULTATION] — SEBI to issue consultation paper on aligning Indian investment rules with global standards

**What happened:**

SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey has said that the market regulator will review the rules that foreign portfolio investors must follow to invest in India. He said SEBI will issue a consultation paper to seek suggestions on bringing Indian rules closer to international standards, while ensuring investor protection and market safety. Mr Pandey was speaking at an Insight Summit in Mumbai yesterday . Acknowledging that some of India’s rules are stricter than those in other major countries, he said, the review aims to make it easier for foreign investors to participate in Indian markets. Mr Pandey said, SEBI has already taken several steps to simplify the process for foreign investors, including easier access to markets, digital registration and simpler procedures. On whether stock exchanges should be allowed to list their own shares on their platforms, Mr Pandey said the idea has neither been rejected nor is it under active consideration. Although some countries allow this practice, India follows a different approach because it has multiple stock exchanges and must guard against possible conflicts of interest. Mr Pandey also stressed that domestic savings will continue to play an important role in developing India’s markets. At the same time, foreign investment can bring in more capital and valuable expertise.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — India’s industrial output grows by 6.7% in September 2026

**What happened:**

India’s industrial output, as measured by the Index of Industrial Production (IIP), grew by 6.7 per cent on an annual basis in September this year. However, the industrial activity slowed on month on month basis, easing from a downwardly revised IIP growth of 7.8 per cent in August. According to the data released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation today, electricity and gas supply recorded strong growth of 11.2 per cent in September, while manufacturing, which carries the heaviest weight in the index, expanded by 7.6 per cent. Water supply, sewerage and waste management also registered healthy growth of 5.8 per cent. In contrast, the mining sector remained a laggard, contracting by 6.2 per cent during the period under review.
