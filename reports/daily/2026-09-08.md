### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · REGULATION] — Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Nasik Jilha Mahila Vikas Sahakari Bank Ltd., Nasik – Extension of Period

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India issued Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to Nasik Jilha Mahila Vikas Sahakari Bank Ltd., Nasik, vide Directive No.CO.DOS.SED.No.S6690/12-22-173/2025-2026 dated December 08, 2025, for a period of six months up to the close of business on June 09, 2026, which were last extended up to the close of business on September 09, 2026 vide Directive No. DOR.MON/D-13/12-22-168/2026-27 dated June 02, 2026. The Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of operation of the Directive beyond close of business on September 09, 2026. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India, in the exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, hereby extends the Directive for a further period of three months from close of business on September 09, 2026, to close of business on December 09, 2026, subject to review. The aforesaid extension and /or modification by the Reserve Bank of India should not per se be construed to imply that the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.

### [MACROECONOMY, TRADE & PUBLIC FINANCE · DATA] — Update: India’s Forex reserves reached a record high of over 740bn US dollars last week

**What happened:**

India’s foreign exchange reserves surged by 11.4 billion US dollars to a record high of 740.8 billion dollars during the week ended 28th of last month. According to the Reserve Bank of India data, Foreign currency assets, the largest component of the reserves, increased by 9.3 billion US dollars to 600.6 billion dollars during the week. Gold reserves added 2.1 billion dollars to 116.4 billion dollars. However, Special Drawing Rights declined by 43 million dollars to 18.8 billion dollars. Country’s reserve position with the International Monetary Fund dipped 11 million dollars to 4.9 billion dollars.
