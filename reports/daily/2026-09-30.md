### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — Government launches Pashudhan Bima Portal to streamline livestock insurance, claims and monitoring

**What happened:**

The Government today launched Pashudhan Bima Portal in New Delhi. The Portal will provide a single, integrated digital platform for livestock insurance, enabling streamlined policy issuance, claim processing, monitoring and reporting in case of animals insured under the Livestock Insurance Scheme of the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying. Speaking on the occasion, Union Minister for Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying Rajiv Ranjan Singh said that this initiative is aimed at making livestock insurance services more transparent, efficient, accessible and technology-driven, while strengthening risk protection for livestock farmers and supporting the sustainability of their livelihoods. More from our correspondent The Pashu Dhan Bima Portal will facilitate better coordination, real-time monitoring and data-driven management of livestock insurance programmes, contributing to improved service delivery across the country. Mr Singh said that this portal is an important step towards protecting the livelihood of farmers and fulfilling the vision of Prime Minister Narendra Modi to increase the farmer’s income, safeguard their agricultural and livestock assets and promote the use of technology. He said through this portal,farmers will now be able to submit their insurance applications online, while the entire process will be monitored digitally. The Portal will also be available in 11 regional languages apart from Hindi and English, enabling livestock farmers to access information and services in languages familiar to them.

### [DIGITAL ECONOMY & AI · INSTITUTIONAL] — Indian Railways Approves New Tri-Weekly Train Service Between Machilipatnam and Kurnool City

**What happened:**

Indian Railways has approved a new tri-weekly train service between Machilipatnam and Kurnool City via Vijayawada of South Coast Railway. This will provide an additional rail connectivity option between coastal and central parts of Andhra Pradesh. The Railways Ministry said that the approved Machilipatnam-Kurnool City Express will cover a distance of nearly five hundred kilometre. The new service will provide an additional travel option for the passengers of Andhra Pradesh and strengthen connectivity between Machilipatnam, Vijayawada and Kurnool City, while also improving access to several towns and cities along the route. The newly launched train will depart from Machilipatnam at 16:55 and reach Kurnool City at 05:45 the next day. In the return direction, the Kurnool City-Machilipatnam Express will depart from Kurnool City at 09:30 AM and reach Machilipatnam at 21:35 the same day. The Machilipatnam-Kurnool City Express will operate on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday, while the Kurnool City-Machilipatnam Express will operate on Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday. The new train will have stoppages at Vijayawada, Gudivada, Narasaraopet, Vinukonda, Donakonda, Markapur Road, Cumbum, Giddalur, Diguvametta, Nandyal, Betamcherla and Dhone.
