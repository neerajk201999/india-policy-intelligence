### [FINANCIAL & BANKING · INSTITUTIONAL] — RBI Receives Over $143.5 Billion Through Special Forex Swap Facility

**What happened:**

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) said its special US dollar-Indian rupee (USD-INR) forex swap facility has received forex inflows of over 143.5 billion US dollars till September 18, 2026. According to the latest data, Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) [FCNR-B] deposits accounted for the overwhelming majority, amounting to 132.98 billion US dollars. The Apex Bank introduced the special USD-INR forex swap facility covering FCNR(B) deposits, ECBs and OFCBs inflows on June 8, 2026, to boost the inflow of dollars amid a weakening rupee. ### [DIGITAL ECONOMY & AI · INSTITUTIONAL] — Government Clarifies UPI MDR Will Not Burden Small Merchants or Make Digital Payments Expensive

**What happened:**

The Government today clarified that GST on UPI Merchant Discount Rate, or MDR, will not burden small merchants or make digital payments expensive. In a social media post, National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has termed such media reports as incorrect. It said, MDR applies only to person-to-merchant transactions above two thousand rupees. It said person-to-merchant transactions up to two thousand rupees continue to have zero MDR and therefore zero GST impact. NPCI stated that transactions of up to two thousand rupees constitute more than 96 per cent of UPI merchant transaction volume. The GST paid on MDR by a merchant will be adjusted against the GST payable on the sale of goods, in the same manner that input taxes are set off against output tax liability. NPCI said that merchants with monthly UPI receipts of up to one lakh rupees are not liable to pay MDR.
